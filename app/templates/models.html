🧠 Model Selection Rationale: Why Each Model Is Used

Brief engineering rationale for the 8 machine learning architectures evaluated in this telemetry system.

Architectural Rationale
🌲 Random Forest Champion • Deployed
Ensemble Bagging (300 Trees)

Why used: Combines 300 de-correlated decision trees to capture multi-sensor interactions (e.g. Temp × Humidity condensation points). Eliminates overfitting and provides supreme noise tolerance for live IoT telemetry.

Champion F1: 99.7% • Low Variance
🌳 Decision Tree White-Box
CART Recursive Splitter

Why used: Complete explainability. Generates transparent, human-readable threshold rules (e.g., CO2 > 350 ppm or Temp > 24°C) to verify that ML logic aligns with post-harvest biological decay science.

F1: 99.7% • Rule Transparent
📍 K-Nearest Neighbors Instance Proximity
Spatial Distance (k=7)

Why used: Local cluster verification. Classifies freshness based on Euclidean distance to historical batches, verifying that identical microclimate regimes reliably produce consistent produce outcomes.

F1: 99.7% • Metric Clustering
🚀 Gradient Boosting Functional Boosting
Sequential Residual Descent

Why used: Fits consecutive shallow trees on pseudo-residuals using gradient descent. Optimizes complex non-linear boundary transitions with low bias and high tabular precision.

F1: 99.6% • Residual Optimization
⚡ SVM (RBF Kernel) Maximum Margin
Kernel Support Vector

Why used: Maximum geometric separation. Projects continuous microclimate telemetry into high-dimensional space via Radial Basis Functions to construct optimal margin boundaries between Fresh and Spoiled states.

F1: 99.6% • Margin Robust
🔥 AdaBoost Adaptive Boosting
Sample Re-weighting Stumps

Why used: Successively re-weights misclassified telemetry samples, forcing subsequent weak decision stumps to focus specifically on hard edge cases and threshold ambiguities.

F1: 98.3% • Adaptive Weights
📈 Logistic Regression Linear Baseline
L2-Regularized Logit

Why used: Parametric reference floor. Evaluates whether spoilage can be resolved by a simple linear boundary, serving as the empirical baseline to measure non-linear gain.

F1: 91.3% • Baseline Floor
📐 Ridge Classifier L2 Closed-Form
Normal Equations Boundary

Why used: Converts binary classification into regularized ridge regression via sign-mapping. Solves closed-form normal equations instantly without iterative gradient descent.

F1: 87.9% • Closed-Form Normal
